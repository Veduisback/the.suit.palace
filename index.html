The philosophy

Tradition, worn forward

The Suite Palace began as a single tailoring table in Patna, three generations ago, where a family of karigars cut wedding sherwanis by lamplight. What hasn't changed is the hand behind the thread. What has, is everything else — silhouette, pace, the women and men who wear it.
